# Privacy Policy for Pitch Me

**Last Updated: November 21, 2025**

## Introduction

Pitch Me ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Pitch Me - AI Pitch Decks (the "App").

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Account Information:** Email address when you create an account
- **User Content:** Pitch decks, slides, text content you create in the App
- **Document Uploads (Pro users):** Files you upload for AI analysis
- **Communication:** Messages you send to our support team

### Automatically Collected Information

- **Usage Data:** How you interact with the App (features used, time spent)
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
- **Crash Data:** Technical data when the App crashes to help us fix bugs

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide and maintain the App
- Generate pitch decks using AI
- Process your subscriptions
- Send important updates about the App
- Improve our services based on usage patterns
- Provide customer support
- Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

## AI Processing

When you use our AI features:
- Your input data is sent to OpenAI (GPT-4/5) for processing
- OpenAI processes your data according to their privacy policy
- We do not train AI models on your personal data
- Your pitch decks remain private to you

## Third-Party Services

We use these trusted third-party services:

- **Firebase (Google):** Authentication, database, cloud functions
- **RevenueCat:** Subscription management
- **OpenAI:** AI content generation
- **Apple:** In-app purchases, analytics

Each service has its own privacy policy governing data use.

## Data Storage & Security

- All data is encrypted in transit using TLS
- Data is stored securely in Firebase Cloud
- We implement industry-standard security measures
- Your payment information is processed by Apple (we never see it)

## Your Data Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access:** Request a copy of your data
- **Delete:** Request deletion of your account and data
- **Export:** Download your pitch decks anytime
- **Opt-out:** Disable analytics tracking (in Settings)

To exercise these rights, email: privacy@pitchme.app

## Data Retention

- Active accounts: Data retained while account is active
- Deleted accounts: Data deleted within 30 days
- Backups: May be retained for up to 90 days
- Legal requirements: We retain data as required by law

## Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## International Users

Your data may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. By using the App, you consent to this transfer.

## Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We'll notify you of significant changes via:
- In-app notification
- Email
- App Store update notes
